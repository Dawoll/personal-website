Music

Rock music

I listen to rock music almost every day. My favourite bands are Nirvana, Foo Fighters and Linkin Park. Among Polish bands, I particularly enjoy Dżem, especially the song Wehikuł Czasu.

Listening to Nirvana inspired me to try playing the guitar, so I bought my first instrument: a Harley Benton MS-60 VW Vintage Series, which I use to learn my first guitar riffs.
