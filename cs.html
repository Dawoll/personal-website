Counter-Strike

My passion for Counter-Strike

I have been playing Counter-Strike since 2015. Although I spend much less time on it now than I used to, I still enjoy coming back to it.

In over 11 years, I have spent nearly 7,000 hours playing. As mentioned on the home page, I have taken part in several school and interschool tournaments,representing my class at high school.

I have also won a few individual online tournaments on FACEIT, playing 1v1 matches for small prizes on third-party websites.

I always dreamed of playing professionally. However, playing irregularly and frequently changing teams, on FACEIT, I reached only around 2500 ELO, which at the time placed me around 2800 in the Polish ranking (TOP ~53,000 EU). I was never completely satisfied with that result.

For a while, I also tried to grow my YouTube channel by posting Shorts featuring my best plays. However, I soon found it difficult to make time for both playing and editing videos.

During my time playing Counter-Strike, I also had the opportunity to play with three professional players, including one from Poland.
